Recommended after installation

Complete MailSeer AI Setup

MailSeer already includes Semantic Search. Two optional local components unlock its strongest ranking and complete Ask MailSeer experience.

Three simple steps

1
Open MailSeer → Local AI
The Local AI panel shows which capabilities are Ready and which still need installation.
2
Install Enhanced Ranking
Strongly recommended. It improves evidence ordering and retrieval precision.
3
Install Local AI
MailSeer installs Ollama and the supported local model for private answers and summaries.

What each capability does

  • Semantic Search finds emails by meaning. Included by default.
  • Enhanced Ranking improves which retrieved emails are considered most relevant.
  • Ask MailSeer turns retrieved email evidence into local answers and summaries.

Search finds the evidence. Enhanced Ranking improves the evidence. Local AI turns it into an answer.

Privacy and performance

  • MailSeer does not require a cloud AI service for Ask MailSeer answers.
  • Local AI runs on your computer after the required one-time downloads.
  • The first answer may take longer while the local model loads.
  • CPU-only systems work, but supported GPUs can improve speed.
  • AI summaries can occasionally be imperfect; the original email remains authoritative.

Windows compatibility

MailSeer 0.2.0 for Windows supports 64-bit Intel and AMD PCs (x64). Windows on Arm is not supported in this release.
